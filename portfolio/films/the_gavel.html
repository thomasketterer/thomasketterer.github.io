The Gavel

I created this for UNCMUNC XV, where I was a part of the secretariat as Director of Logistics. As part of my duties I was tasked with coming up with the proceedings of the closing ceremony. From this came the idea for 'The Gavel', a video to get people excited and ready for the end of the weekend.
