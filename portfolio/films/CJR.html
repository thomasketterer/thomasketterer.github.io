Carolina Jump Rope Showcase

Each year the Collegiate Jump Rope Championship brings teams together from all across the nation to compete in the sport of jump rope. Part of the final team score is determined by a 'showcase video' where teams present a curated view of their skills, tricks, and team coordination in a fun manner. I volunteered to create this video, scripting, blocking, filming, editing, and starring in the it. At the compeition, the video propelled our team to a second place finish with the showcase itself winning first!
