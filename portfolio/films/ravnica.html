A Crisis on Ravnica Primer

Throghout my four years in university I spent a lot of my time in Model UN committees, crisis directing five and helping with three others. As my final committee, my friend Johnny and I took on the challenge of a Magic the Gathering themed Ad-Hoc Special committee. We knew that the mechanics of the committee would be hard for delegates to comprehend so I took on the responsibility of creating this video. We then played it before committee started to help explain to the delegates how everything works. This was the first time that I worked with greenscreen and set building, which made it a unique challenge and allowed me to learn a lot during the short production.
