Media Noche

The inspiration for this project came from the directors, Vanessa Sanders-Taboada, perseonal history with Puerto Rico. I was brought on an editor, editing Skit 2 (the surreal sequence with the horses) and other VFX within the short film. This was my second experience working with green screens, which remain as tricky as ever, and the first time I really got to showcase my VFX skills. This was the first time I worked on a film where I was not the lead decision maker, and I learned a lot about balancing your personal vision with others.
